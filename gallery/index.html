Designed once, made once. This piece began as a written intention, was resolved by the atelier's design engine into a technical flat, and was cut from reclaimed cloth by a single pair of hands. No size run, no restock — the pattern is retired the day it ships.

Every panel carries the history of the garment it used to be. Seams are finished open, hardware is recovered, and the dye lot will never repeat. Made for one. Not for all.
