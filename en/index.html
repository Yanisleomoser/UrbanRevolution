AI, done right

AI can speed up the line. Or close the circle.

On the line

Predict, push, repeat

Recommendation engines tuned to make you buy the next thing, faster. AI that feeds the line — manufacturing new wants.

In the circle

See, separate, reclaim

AI aimed at the discard pile: it is meant to untangle what was thrown away so it can live again.

The hard problem in the circle was never the sewing — it is untangling the discarded: mixed fibres, fastenings, seams, dyes. A pile no human line can sort at scale. That is exactly where the machine is meant to work: sort the fabric and build the piece. Nothing more. The design stays yours — you set the style, you make every decision. The AI never designs.

    [ FAQ ]

    FAQ

    Everything you want to know before you join the vision.

    How long does production take?

    Nothing is made yet — we're building the factory that makes it possible. Once production starts, we'll state binding timelines here. Until then: no promises we can't keep.

    What happens to my photo?

    For measurement detection everything runs directly in your browser via MediaPipe — the photo never leaves your device and isn't stored. Only if you explicitly request the photorealistic preview is the photo sent once to Replicate (US servers) and immediately discarded after generation.

    What does a piece cost?

    Not set yet — prices will exist only once we actually manufacture. Be there and you'll be the first to know.

    What if I haven't been measured?

    Three ways to your measurements — photo analysis (1 click, 100% client-side), manual entry of the 9 values, or a size preset (S/M/L/XL) as a starting point you can adjust afterwards. Your measurements belong to your design and are ready the moment manufacturing starts.

    Where is it made?

    In the planned fully autonomous circular factory that sorts discarded textiles and makes your one-of-a-kind piece from them. Sign up — you'll be the first to know when and where it happens.

    Can I order saved designs later?

    Your designs stay saved in your library (locally in your browser). You'll be able to order them once we go live — just bring them back up again.
